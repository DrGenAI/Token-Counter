Privacy Policy

Token Counter for ChatGPT · last updated 15 September 2026

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no server, no analytics, and no third-party service of any kind.

What it reads

To count the tokens in your conversation, the extension reads:

  • The text of the ChatGPT conversation open in your browser tab.
  • Responses from chatgpt.com's own endpoints that the page has already requested, to read conversation contents and usage figures.
  • The authorization header the ChatGPT page attaches to its own requests. This is held in memory only, used solely to re-issue requests to chatgpt.com on the same origin, and never stored or sent anywhere else.

All of this happens locally in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

What it stores

Your display preferences only — context limit, and whether each element is shown. These are kept in Chrome's extension storage and synced across your own signed-in Chrome profiles by Chrome itself. They contain no conversation data.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension makes no requests to any domain other than chatgpt.com, and those go only to endpoints the ChatGPT page itself already uses.

Permissions

  • storage — saves your display preferences.
  • Host access to chatgpt.com — required because counting a conversation means reading that conversation. The extension runs on no other site.

Changes

Any change to this policy ships with a new version of the extension and is reflected in the date above.
